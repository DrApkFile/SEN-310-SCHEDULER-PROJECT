Overview

Project Description

Solace Wellness Spot Scheduler is a Django web application that manages appointment booking for a wellness spa offering massage, acupuncture, facials, and yoga sessions. It coordinates three roles — Client, Practitioner, and Admin/Manager — through role-specific dashboards. Clients browse services and book time slots that are validated in real time against a practitioner's declared weekly availability and existing bookings. Every appointment moves through a lifecycle (pending → confirmed/declined → completed/cancelled), with each transition triggering an in-app notification and an email. Admins get a reporting dashboard covering revenue, booking status breakdowns, and popular services, and can provision new practitioner accounts.

Participants

Actors

Client

A registered customer who browses services, checks live availability, books or cancels appointments, and tracks their upcoming and past sessions.

Practitioner

Spa staff (therapist, esthetician, instructor) who defines weekly working hours, reviews incoming requests, and accepts, declines, or completes bookings.

Admin / Manager

Business owner or manager who monitors revenue and booking metrics, and onboards new practitioner accounts with assigned specialties.
